About

I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at the Georgia Institute of Technology, specializing in Computing Systems, following my undergraduate studies at Boston College, where I earned a B.A. in Computer Science and Mathematics.

My interests lie at the intersection of AI/ML systems, computer vision, and applied machine learning. I am particularly interested in building efficient, scalable, and reliable systems for modern AI workloads, as well as developing computer-vision and learning-based methods for scientific and biomedical applications.

Through my research, I have worked on deep learning, image segmentation, biomedical imaging, large-scale image analysis, research software, and AI-assisted annotation tools. These experiences motivate my broader interest in machine learning systems and AI infrastructure that make data-intensive applications more efficient and practical. I am interested in connecting with researchers and engineers in these areas and in related research and internship opportunities.
